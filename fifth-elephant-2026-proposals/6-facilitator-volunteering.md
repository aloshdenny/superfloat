## Where I am useful

Three areas where I have enough scar tissue to be worth talking to.

**Evaluation design.** Specifically the failure where a metric rewards a system that has stopped working, and the more general question of what liveness number belongs beside a capability number. I found this the hard way on a benchmark that scored a broken model at 100 percent.

**Model compression and numerical precision.** What it costs, when it is free, why it sometimes fails at sixteen bits when it worked at four, and how to check your own checkpoint before committing to anything.

**Designing a sweep that can falsify your own claim.** I have retired two of my own published results this year, and I am useful for the specific job of turning "I noticed X" into an experiment that could actually show X is false.

I am also happy to do the unglamorous version of this: taking someone's rough problem card and asking the four questions that make it answerable.
