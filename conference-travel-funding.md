# Speaker travel and visa support, checked 2026-09-29
For an Indian passport holder. "Funded" means the conference reimburses airfare or accommodation.

## Direct answers

**40C3: yes, partially.** The CfP says "If necessary, we are able to provide limited
support for travel costs." Full talk speakers get free admission. It is a non
commercial event, nobody is paid, and the support is discretionary rather than a
published amount. They also issue official invitation letters for the German
embassy if you ask the content team early, and they warn that visa processing can
take six weeks. Deadline Oct 9, event Dec 27 to 30. Schengen visa needed.

**SCaLE 24x: no.** Quoted exactly: "SCaLE is a totally volunteer run, sponsor
supported conference. We're unable to provide financial assistance to speakers."
Speakers get a complimentary full conference pass and nothing else. Nothing on the
page about visa letters. A US B1/B2 plus flights to Los Angeles, entirely self
funded. Deadline Nov 1, event Apr 1 to 4 2027.
